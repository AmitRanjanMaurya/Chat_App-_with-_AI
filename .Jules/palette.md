## 2024-05-24 - Accessible Interactive Icons in Jetpack Compose
**Learning:** Using `Modifier.clickable` on an `Icon` component in Jetpack Compose does not automatically provide the necessary minimum touch target size (48x48dp) or visual feedback (like a ripple effect) required for good accessibility and UX.
**Action:** Always prefer wrapping `Icon` components with an `IconButton` when they are meant to be interactive. `IconButton` built-in defaults satisfy accessibility touch targets and provide focus semantics and ripple effects out of the box.
## 2024-05-25 - Disabling IconButtons and Icon Tints in Jetpack Compose
**Learning:** When disabling an `IconButton` in Jetpack Compose to provide visual feedback for invalid states (e.g., empty form), any hardcoded custom `tint` on the inner `Icon` (like `MaterialTheme.colorScheme.primary`) does not automatically adapt to the disabled state, and alternative valid states (like image attachments) must be accounted for.
**Action:** Dynamically calculate the enablement state (e.g., `isSendEnabled = text.isNotBlank() || image != null`) and explicitly adjust the inner `Icon`'s `tint` (e.g., to `MaterialTheme.colorScheme.onSurface.copy(alpha = 0.38f)` when disabled) to provide accurate accessibility cues.
